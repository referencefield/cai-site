# AI Methodology Disclosure

This corpus was developed with the assistance of large language model (LLM) systems, including Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI), Gemini (Google), and others, used as research, drafting, editorial, and adversarial review tools throughout the production process.

All analytical frameworks, structural arguments, terminology, and architectural claims represent the intellectual work and editorial judgment of Reference Field, Inc. AI-assisted tools were used to accelerate drafting, stress-test arguments, and identify gaps, not to generate or substitute for the substantive positions the corpus advances.

The corpus has been reviewed, revised, and approved by Reference Field, Inc. prior to publication. No AI system is responsible for the content of this corpus, and no AI system's outputs are presented here without human review and editorial judgment.
